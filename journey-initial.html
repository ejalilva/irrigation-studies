Sharif University of Technology

Tehran, Iran

My master’s and PhD studies at Sharif focused on water resources. A course in GIS and remote sensing sparked my interest in observing water from space.

Italy

Research collaboration

An email to Luca Brocca led to research in Italy with him as my co-advisor. We explored how soil moisture remembers rainfall—and later realized the approach could also reveal irrigation.

Michigan State University

East Lansing, USA · 2022

After completing my PhD in 2019, I kept building collaborations around irrigation. Work with Ronnie Abolafia Rosenzweig and Narendra Das eventually led to a postdoctoral position at Michigan State and my move to the U.S. in 2022.

NASA Goddard

Greenbelt, Maryland, USA

A collaboration with Sujay Kumar opened the door to NASA Goddard. My research asks how satellite observations can help bring human decisions about water use into large-scale water-cycle models.

Curiosity, collaboration, and a few unexpected turns.
