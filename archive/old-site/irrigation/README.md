# Old Irrigation page: pictures and embeds

Everything that was on the Google Sites page
[ehsanjalilvand.com/research/irrigation](https://www.ehsanjalilvand.com/research/irrigation), copied on 31 October 2026.
This folder is a record and is **not** published; the website uses its own copies in `site/assets/irrigation/`.

Pictures are the largest size Google Sites serves (up to 1280 px wide). Large PNGs and JPEGs were saved as WebP. The two
animated GIFs were 12.6 MB and 2.5 MB, so they are kept as short MP4 loops instead. Left out: the four round "1–4"
number badges, and a meme with a film still of a public figure that sat beside the climate-change bullets.
