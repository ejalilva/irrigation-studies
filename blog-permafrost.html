When the ice wall breaks

Permafrost, ancient carbon and the power of a familiar story.

Ehsan Jalilvand10 January 2023 · 1 min read

At AGU 2022, I used the ice-wall scene from Game of Thrones to start a conversation about thawing permafrost. The frozen ground became the wall; the carbon stored within it became the army waiting on the other side.

The article connects that analogy to my work on satellite soil moisture in northern latitudes, and reflects on the conversations it sparked with scientists from other fields.

The Ice wall (permafrost) is going to break and an army of the dead (ancient carbon) is going to be released!
