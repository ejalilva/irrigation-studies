My journey

2013 · Sharif University. PhD: rainfall estimated from changes in soil moisture, and soil hydraulic properties from soil-moisture drydowns.

Congress paper 2015: Estimating the rate of evaporation from Lake Urmia in 1995 using satellite and gauge data

2015–19 · My startup. Co-founded a startup for environmental monitoring from satellite observations.

2016–17 · CNR IRPI, Italy. Worked on improving SM2RAIN, which estimates rainfall from soil moisture.

J. Hydrol. 2018: Estimating the drainage rate from surface soil moisture drydowns: application of the DfD model

2019 · Sharif University. Finished my PhD with my first irrigation study, and taught undergraduate hydrology.
