Research foundations

2013

Sharif University

Learning to see the Earth differently

My PhD began at Sharif in 2013. My early introduction to GIS and remote sensing gave me a new way to ask questions about water. I started with ArcGIS and began learning MATLAB.

Skills added

  • GIS & ArcGIS
  • Remote sensing
  • MATLAB foundations

What this enabled Mapping and interpreting satellite observations.

Entrepreneurship

2015–2019

Building a startup

Turning an idea into a product

Alongside my PhD, I co-founded a company in 2015. Courses on entrepreneurship helped us move from ideas to products and minimum viable products. I designed website experiences for satellite-based agricultural monitoring and rural tourism, using PowerPoint to prototype interfaces and create presentation videos. We dissolved the company in 2019, but the design and communication skills stayed with me.

Skills added

  • MVP development
  • UX prototyping
  • Visual storytelling

What this enabled Product concepts, two website UX designs and presentation videos.

Research collaboration

2016–2017

CNR IRPI · Italy

From scripts to reusable models

In Italy, I strengthened my MATLAB skills while processing satellite imagery. I learned to build models, write functions and assemble reusable components into modular software. Coding became a way to develop and test scientific ideas.

Skills added

  • Satellite image processing
  • Model development
  • Modular programming

What this enabled Reusable functions and satellite-processing workflows.

Research & computing

2020

First postdoctoral role

From satellite data to land models

My first postdoctoral role introduced me to the VIC model, land surface modeling and data assimilation. I began working in Ubuntu and Linux, using the command line and Bash scripts. I used MATLAB to preprocess satellite data and prepare model inputs.

Skills added

  • Linux & Ubuntu
  • Command line & Bash
  • VIC & land surface modeling
  • Data assimilation

What this enabled Satellite data prepared for modeling and assimilation experiments.

Teaching & faculty

2020–2021

Teaching & Earth science

Making knowledge accessible online

In 2020 and 2021, I taught at Amirkabir University and learned Google Earth Engine while teaching a master’s course. Teaching through the pandemic also meant learning to record lessons, which led me to start a YouTube channel. In 2021, I became a faculty member at the Research Institute for Earth Sciences, before moving to the United States.

Skills added

  • Google Earth Engine
  • Recorded teaching
  • YouTube communication

What this enabled Online lessons, a YouTube channel and an Earth-science faculty role.

Postdoctoral research

2022

Michigan State University

Scaling up—and moving to Python

At Michigan State, I was introduced to high-performance computing. My online master’s studies in analytics at Georgia Tech also helped me learn Python. I moved from MATLAB toward Python, building confidence with NumPy and pandas, then expanding into machine learning with scikit-learn and PyTorch.

Skills added

  • High-performance computing
  • Python
  • NumPy & pandas
  • scikit-learn
  • PyTorch

What this enabled Larger computing workflows and a growing Python and machine-learning toolkit.

Research scientist

2023–present

NASA Goddard

Building research software and global products

At NASA, I deepened my use of Git and GitHub, developed within NASA’s Land Information System (LIS) using Fortran, and learned to build Python software packages through AgroTrack. I developed my first global soil-moisture product using machine learning. The work expanded both the scientific questions I could tackle and the software skills I needed to answer them.

Skills added

  • Git & GitHub
  • NASA LIS & Fortran
  • AgroTrack Python package
  • Global ML products

What this enabled A global machine-learning soil-moisture product and reusable research software.
