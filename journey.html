My journey

2013 · Sharif University. PhD: an inverse water balance model (SM2RAIN-DfD) that estimates rainfall from satellite soil moisture alone, and soil hydraulic properties from soil-moisture drydowns.

Congress paper 2015: Estimating the rate of evaporation from Lake Urmia in 1995 using satellite and gauge data

2015–19 · SDK, my startup. Co-founded a startup for environmental monitoring from satellite observations.

2016–17 · CNR IRPI, Italy. Visiting researcher, improving SM2RAIN, a bottom-up approach that estimates rainfall from satellite soil moisture.

J. Hydrol. 2018: Estimating the drainage rate from surface soil moisture drydowns: application of the DfD model

2019 · Sharif University. Finished my PhD with my first irrigation study, and taught undergraduate hydrology.

RSE 2019: Quantification of irrigation water using remote sensing of soil moisture in a semi-arid region

2019–21 · First postdoc, Sharif University. Detected the irrigation signal in SMAP soil moisture, and assimilated SMAP–Sentinel-1 soil moisture into the VIC model to estimate how much water farms applied.

IEEE JSTARS 2021: Evaluation of SMAP/Sentinel-1 high-resolution soil moisture data to detect irrigationSustainability 2021: Estimating soil moisture and mapping irrigated cropland with Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2WRR 2023: Is it possible to quantify irrigation water-use by assimilating a high-resolution satellite soil moisture product?

2021–22 · Amirkabir University. Taught a graduate course on remote sensing and GIS in water resources, recorded for YouTube.

Lessons on YouTube: Recorded lessons: remote sensing and GIS in water resources

2021–22 · Research Institute for Earth Sciences, Geological Survey of Iran. Assistant professor: a rainfall-triggered landslide risk map of Iran, and climate-change hotspots from high-resolution climate models.

Climate 2022: Using high-resolution climate models to identify climate change hotspots in the Middle East: a case study of Iran

2022–23 · Second postdoc, Michigan State University. Built high-resolution SMAP–Sentinel-1 soil moisture for northern latitudes and permafrost, brought human water use into Noah-MP through data assimilation, worked on NISAR soil moisture for field-scale irrigation, and joined NASA JPL’s SMAPVEX22 field campaign.

COMPAG 2024: Yield estimation from SAR data using patch-based deep learning and machine learning techniquesISPRS J. 2024: Review of synthetic aperture radar with deep learning in agricultural applicationsFront. Earth Sci. 2026: Low-latency, high-resolution SMAP soil moisture retrievals for near-real-time applications

2023 · Georgia Tech, online. Began an online Master of Science in Analytics.

2023 · NASA Goddard. Water cycle and Earth system modeling, bringing satellite observations and machine learning into the models.

NASA LIS on GitHub: NASA-LIS/LISF: the Land Information System Framework (contributor)RSE 2026 · AgroTrack: Characterization of irrigation timing using thermal satellite observations, a data-driven approachGeoStreamPy on GitHub: GeoStreamPy: stream Google Earth Engine data into xarrayWRR 2026: Beyond SMAP: can GNSS-R platforms ensure continuity of global soil moisture observations?
